# Local Info

Train departures and today's weather for one place, served to an LED display.

## Weather

**Temperature**:
The measured air temperature, in °C.
_Avoid_: Temp (when it could mean feels-like), actual temperature

**Feels-like temperature**:
How warm or cold the air feels once wind chill and humidity are taken into account, in °C.
_Avoid_: Apparent temperature, real feel

**Hourly series**:
24 values for today, one per clock hour from 00:00 to 23:00, including hours that have already passed.
_Avoid_: Forecast hours, remaining hours

**Daily high / Daily low**:
The highest and lowest temperature over the whole of today, from 00:00 to 23:59, including hours that have already passed.
_Avoid_: Max/min (unqualified), today's remaining high
